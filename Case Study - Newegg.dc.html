Newegg Commerce

Newegg, a leading retailer in computing hardware and consumer electronics, faced considerable challenges related to time-consuming manual processes and inconsistent freight rates. To address these issues, Newegg turned to FreightPOP, an advanced Transportation Management System (TMS) aimed at streamlining logistics operations. This case study illustrates how FreightPOP transformed Newegg’s shipping processes, boosting efficiency and visibility while significantly enhancing time management. As a result, Newegg was able to provide superior service to its customers and lower operational costs.

About Newegg

Newegg, established in 2001, has grown into a comprehensive online retailer offering a vast range of electronics and tech products. Known initially for computer hardware, it has expanded its catalog to include everything from gaming consoles, laptops, and TVs to home essentials like vacuums, kitchen appliances, and even camping gear.

This extensive selection caters to both casual shoppers and tech enthusiasts, making Newegg a go-to destination for diverse product needs. With detailed product information, user reviews, and frequent deals and promotions, Newegg remains a valuable resource for anyone keeping up with the latest in technology and beyond.
