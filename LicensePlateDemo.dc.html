Identifying and tracking inventory
Three ways to identify inventory,
set at the warehouse level.
License plate (LPN)
A physical container or group of inventory
Track and move inventory as a unit. Auto-generated from a prefix and starting number, required on every receiving allocation line, and split automatically when you pick less than the full plate.
Lot tracking
A batch or group of like inventory
Maintain batch-level traceability, with lot expiration for shelf-life goods. Empty lot ids can default to today's date, the PO number or a fixed value — a lot id typed by hand is never overwritten.
Serialization
One individual item
Maintain unit-level traceability. Serials import from the ERP, are viewed and edited from Product Details on Quote/Ship, and can print on the BOL and packing slip — grouped by stop on multi-stop loads.
Enabled per warehouse, then mandatory on every transaction in it.
