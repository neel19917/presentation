4Wall Entertainment

4Wall Entertainment, a leader in entertainment services across North America and Europe, faced challenges in managing regional carrier selections, integrating existing carriers, and tracking equipment in real time. Partnering with FreightPOP, 4Wall implemented a Transportation Management System that provided regional control over carrier usage, seamless integration with existing carriers, and real-time tracking capabilities. This collaboration resulted in streamlined shipping processes, enhanced customer service, and reduced processing times.

Challenges & Solutions

Three bottlenecks, three fixes

Before
With FreightPOP
01

Limited regional carrier control

Less experienced team members struggled to identify the best carriers for specific shipments.

01

Regional Carrier Selection Control

Customizable views inside FreightPOP's TMS restrict or allow specific carriers by region, so staff always select the most appropriate carrier for each area.

02

Difficult FTL and LTL integration

The team needed a seamless way to switch between truckload and less-than-truckload modes based on shipment needs.

02

Integration with Existing Carriers

Support for integrations with over 300 carriers let 4Wall keep their existing carrier relationships and manage both FTL and LTL shipments through a unified dashboard.

03

No real-time tracking visibility

Inability to monitor shipments in real time caused inefficiencies in equipment movement between events.

03

Enhanced Real-Time Tracking

Comprehensive inbound and outbound tracking gives at-a-glance visibility of equipment locations — no logging into multiple carrier websites, no delayed email notifications.
