The Challenges

Four bottlenecks holding freight back

01

Excel-Based Freight Operations

Freight operations were managed across spreadsheets with no standardized workflow from order to shipment. BOLs, tracking, and carrier assignments were all manual, creating error risk, operational inconsistency, and limited scalability as volumes and locations grew.

02

Manual Broker and Carrier Management

Refrigerated brokers and carriers were managed through email and Excel, with no centralized view of rates, performance, or historical trends. Each load required manual quoting and selection, slowing decisions and preventing meaningful optimization of carriers and freight spend.

03

Dock Scheduling Bottlenecks

Dock scheduling was managed through spreadsheets and manual coordination, even as a new facility came online. With limited visibility into dock utilization, warehouse teams faced congestion risk, appointment conflicts, and ongoing inefficiencies in daily operations. The team needed a dock scheduling solution — either built into their transportation platform or capable of integrating with a standalone dock system — both of which FreightPOP supports.

04

Integration Gaps with NetSuite & AP

While NetSuite was the system of record, transportation workflows lived outside of it. Freight invoices were manually processed, audits were time-consuming, and shipment data was disconnected from costs — making it difficult to report confidently on freight KPIs or manage spend at scale.

Custom Workflow Solution

Three connected workflows

NetSuite-Integrated TMS for LTL & FTL

Order → Shipment → Cost → NetSuite

Sales Order to Shipment Planning. Orders flow from NetSuite into FreightPOP for carrier selection, rate shopping, and execution.

Item Fulfillment Sync. Shipment updates are sent back to NetSuite as completed fulfillments.

Bidirectional Data Sync. Ship-to details, customer PO numbers, product data, and custom fields remain aligned between systems.

Automated Freight Cost Posting. Carrier details, tracking numbers, PODs, and shipping costs are automatically pushed into NetSuite.

Carrier & Broker Management

Rate Discovery → Tendering → Tracking → Performance Review

Centralized Rate Shopping. Rates across brokers and carriers are searched and compared in one system.

Direct Tendering. Loads are tendered electronically to carriers or brokers.

Consolidated Tracking. Shipment statuses and exceptions are visible in one place.

Performance Analytics. On-time performance, utilization, and exceptions are tracked over time.

Freight Audit & AP Automation

Invoice Receipt → Audit → Vendor Bill → Reporting

Automated Invoice Matching. Invoices are matched to loads and contracted rates.

Exception-Based Review. Variances are flagged before approval.

NetSuite Vendor Bills. Approved charges post automatically to NetSuite.

Audit-Ready Freight Data. Costs remain tied to shipments and orders for reporting.

Business Impact

Business Impact

01

Operational Efficiency & Time Savings

By replacing Excel with an integrated TMS, Miami Beef streamlined shipment planning and eliminated duplicate data entry across NetSuite. Instead of relying on disconnected spreadsheets and manual coordination, the team now manages transportation in a single system — reducing repetitive administrative work and freeing up time to focus on exceptions and customer service.

02

Data Quality & Reporting

Integrated workflows ensure consistent capture of shipment, product, and freight data in NetSuite, automatically linking costs, tracking, and carriers. This enables reliable freight KPI reporting and advanced analytics while reducing manual reconciliation between operations and finance.

03

Freight Optimization & Audit Control

Automated rate shopping and freight audit capabilities support better carrier selection and give AP a structured audit process with clear exception handling. Freight spend is better controlled without adding manual workload.
