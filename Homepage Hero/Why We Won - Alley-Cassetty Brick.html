Alley-Cassetty Brick & Stone

A 145-year-old building materials manufacturer and distributor in Nashville, TN — 15 locations, 115+ delivery trucks, nearly 50,000 deliveries a year. All of it, until FreightPOP, on paper.

“Streamlining the process and creating consistency among all the locations is key.” — Suzi Johnson

The Challenge
01

No inventory visibility

No way to track inventory movement, bin locations, or stock levels across 15 warehouses — operating completely blind on the warehouse side.

02

Zero warehouse standardization

Each of 15 yards ran its own ad hoc process — pegboards, Excel, Word — with no consistent picking, slotting, or bin management.

03

Paper-based pick tickets

Dispatch tickets printed out of SYSPRO with no digital handoff to the yard floor, creating delays and loading errors every shift.

04

Partial loads, returns, and reps in the dark

Untracked truck capacity and job site pickups leaked revenue, while sales reps had no self-serve view of order status.

Why FreightPOP Won

WMS with real bin management

Pegboards and paper replaced by live inventory visibility and consistent pick workflows across all 15 locations.

Native SYSPRO integration

Order data flows straight into FreightPOP, turning printed tickets into digital dispatch tied live to the order system.

Partial load & return tracking

Dispatchers see truck capacity utilization, ending the revenue leakage from untracked job site pickups.

Self-serve visibility for reps

Order and fulfillment status without a dispatcher — one source of truth from yard floor to delivery truck.
