Once Upon a Farm

Once Upon a Farm, a leader in organic food products, faced a critical need to streamline and enhance their shipping processes. Facing reliability and spend issues, they required a reliable TMS (Transportation Management System) solution to support logistics, integrate with their Sage X3 accounting system, and maintain full visibility over their shipments. After facing past challenges with vendors who failed to deliver on commitments, Once Upon a Farm sought a solution that would not only meet their immediate needs but also provide long-term value.

About Once Upon a Farm

Once Upon a Farm is a pioneering company in the organic food industry, dedicated to providing fresh, nutritious, and wholesome food options for families. Known for their commitment to quality and sustainability, they produce minimally processed products made from organic, farm-fresh ingredients.

With a mission to make real, healthy food accessible to all, Once Upon a Farm offers a variety of refrigerated snacks and meals, designed with both taste and nutrition in mind. As they grow, the company remains rooted in values of transparency, responsibility, and a dedication to supporting organic farming and environmental stewardship.

Growth outpaced the process

As Once Upon a Farm experienced rapid growth, expanding their distribution channels and reaching more retailers nationwide, their shipping and logistics demands grew significantly. Managing increased order volumes, coordinating with multiple carriers, and ensuring timely deliveries became more complex and resource-intensive. This growth highlighted the need for a robust Transportation Management System that could streamline their shipping operations.
