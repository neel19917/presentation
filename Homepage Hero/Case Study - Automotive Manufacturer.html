Global Automotive Manufacturer

A leading Japanese automotive manufacturer was facing growing challenges in managing its complex global shipping operations. With a vast supply chain involving multiple vendors and distribution centers, coordinating shipments across global partners became increasingly difficult — resulting in rising costs, frequent delays, and limited visibility. To regain control and optimize performance, they implemented a centralized transportation management solution that could seamlessly integrate with their SAP S/4HANA ERP, supporting streamlined, multi-mode, and scalable shipping.
