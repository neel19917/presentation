Document Technology  ·  Parcel & Freight

Kyocera Document Solutions America

Kyocera Document Solutions America (KDA) faced several challenges across its five U.S. facilities, which include three regional distribution hubs, a parts distribution center, and a national returns center. The company sought a comprehensive solution to eliminate manual shipping processes and improve shipment tracking. To address these needs, KDA turned to FreightPOP, a cloud-based Transportation Management System (TMS), to streamline its freight lifecycle and optimize shipping operations.
