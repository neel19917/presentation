--- MODULE 01 · Shipping Rules Engine ---
Hub card title: Shipping Rules Engine
Card tag: Codify shipping logic — and let qualifying orders book themselves

STEP 01 · THE PROBLEM
Heading: Every rep ships by their own playbook.
Body: Which carrier for hazmat, when a guaranteed service is required, which account to bill — that logic lives in people's heads. New hires guess, veterans improvise, and every routine order still waits on a human to click ship.

STEP 02 · THE BENEFIT
Heading: Turn tribal knowledge into rules that fire automatically.
  • Route by weight, value, destination, hazmat, customer or SLA
  • Big-box and collect freight route to the customer's carrier and account automatically
  • Force guaranteed service on critical lanes; block non-compliant carriers
  • Auto dispatch: orders that satisfy a rule quote, book and label with no human touch
  • Crawl-walk-run — automate the low-hanging lanes first, then widen the rules

STEP 03 · LIVE DEMO
Caption: A routing rule is defined once — then quotes, books and dispatches every qualifying shipment on its own.
Demo: rules  · AI demo: copilot  · Live: https://app.freightpop.com/app/#/company/rules?tab=shipping-approval-rule

Evidence grade: Platform
Proof: Policy is enforced on every shipment automatically — and the orders that qualify never wait on a person.

--- MODULE 02 · License Plating, Lot, and Serialization ---
Card tag: Move a pallet, not a serial number

STEP 01 · THE PROBLEM
Heading: Serialized and lot-controlled stock moves one scan at a time.
Body: A pallet of serialized units can't move until someone scans every serial on it, and lot codes live on paper or in a spreadsheet — so expiry, recall and traceability questions turn into a warehouse walk. Multi-section warehouses relocate stock all day, so the scanning never ends, or worse, it just gets skipped.

STEP 02 · THE BENEFIT
Heading: One license plate carries the whole pallet.
  • Associate items, serials and lot codes to a single scannable license plate
  • One scan moves the pallet — every serial and lot code travels with it

The Challenges

Four bottlenecks holding freight back

01

Excel-Based Freight Operations

Freight operations were managed across spreadsheets with no standardized workflow from order to shipment. BOLs, tracking, and carrier assignments were all manual, creating error risk, operational inconsistency, and limited scalability as volumes and locations grew.

02

Manual Broker and Carrier Management

Refrigerated brokers and carriers were managed through email and Excel, with no centralized view of rates, performance, or historical trends. Each load required manual quoting and selection, slowing decisions and preventing meaningful optimization of carriers and freight spend.

03

Dock Scheduling Bottlenecks

Dock scheduling was managed through spreadsheets and manual coordination, even as a new facility came online. With limited visibility into dock utilization, warehouse teams faced congestion risk, appointment conflicts, and ongoing inefficiencies in daily operations. The team needed a dock scheduling solution — either built into their transportation platform or capable of integrating with a standalone dock system — both of which FreightPOP supports.

04

Integration Gaps with NetSuite & AP

While NetSuite was the system of record, transportation workflows lived outside of it. Freight invoices were manually processed, audits were time-consuming, and shipment data was disconnected from costs — making it difficult to report confidently on freight KPIs or manage spend at scale.
