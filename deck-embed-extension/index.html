Keep FreightPOP inside the deck.

Some actions in the live app — the truck icon on History, exports — open a new browser tab and pull you out of the presentation. This small browser extension keeps them inside the deck: they open as a new tab in the deck's Live Site strip instead. It only runs when the app is framed by the deck; normal use of app.freightpop.com is unchanged, and nothing is read or sent anywhere.

Download the helper (zip) ↓Read the notes

Install once, about 30 seconds

  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder named deck-embed-extension.
  2. In Chrome, Edge or Brave open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick that folder.
  4. Reload the deck. In a module's Live Site tab open History and click a truck icon: it opens as a new deck tab, and the Live Site toolbar shows Helper on.

Keep the folder where it is; the browser loads the extension from there. If the FreightPOP app later ships the in-deck change natively, remove the extension.
